Production Process & Shipping

Watch how we make your apparel, and see how fast we can get it to your door.

See Our Production Line

From digital proofing to final packing, quality is checked at every step.

1. Design & Proofing

Our designers check your artwork for resolution, color separation, and print readiness.

2. Cutting & Prepping

Garments are laid out, checked for seams, and prepped for the printing line.

3. Screen Printing

High-precision machines apply the ink, ensuring colors pop and stay vibrant.

4. Embroidery

Multi-head embroidery machines stitch logos with precision and consistency.

5. Quality Check

Every single piece is inspected for flaws, misprints, and stitching errors.

6. Folding & Packing

Sorted by size and securely packaged for transit, ready for express shipping.

Shipping & Logistics

We guarantee delivery before your event. Track your order every step of the way.

🌐 Global Express Delivery

We partner with DHL, FedEx, and UPS to ensure fast, reliable international shipping. All orders come with a tracking number sent directly to your email.

🛡️ On-Time Guarantee

If your order misses the guaranteed delivery date due to our production or shipping delays, we will refund your expedited shipping fees entirely.

Every Option, Explained

See the printing process in action and feel the fabric quality before you order.

Printing Methods in Action

Screen Printing

Bold, high-opacity designs. Best value at 50+ pcs and highly durable after washing.

DTG Printing

Direct-to-garment full-color printing. No color limits, ideal for highly detailed artwork.

Embroidery

Premium stitched logos on polos, caps, and jackets. Unbeatable durability and texture.

Heat Transfer

Perfect for lightweight custom names and numbers. Great for small batches and personalization.

Foil Printing

Metallic gold or silver finishes for premium brand logos. Adds a luxury feel to any event.

Custom Labels

Personalized woven neck labels and care tags to complete your custom brand identity.
